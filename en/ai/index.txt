We think the future of QA is agents testing themselves, not writing scripts.<p>Mo is a custom agent harness for catching bugs that builds on the past 3 years of Momentic and millions of test traces from complex, real-world applications. Mo is built for recall: it finds the most real bugs, though Codex is a bit more precise at 86% vs 84%. Large product areas automatically fork into parallel sub-agents, each with their own browser or device for concurrent execution.<p>- Specialized agents for domain specific tasks. Planner agents reason over screenshots and video while dedicated locator agents inspect page structure to resolve plain-English interaction targets. Others include image analysis, video understanding, audio generation, and more.<p>- Parallel agents coordinate through cooperative resource leases. Agents can announce that they’re “using” or “modifying” overlapping resources or fixtures. Multiple “using” claims can coexist, but only a single “modifying” claim can be active on any particular one.<p>- Scaled infrastructure.
