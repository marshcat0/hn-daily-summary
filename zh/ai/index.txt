We even use marimo pair to find and fix bugs in itself and marimo [5]. In these examples the notebook is not only a computational substrate but also a canvas for collaboration between humans and agents, and an executable, literate artifact comprised of prose, code, and visuals.<p>marimo pair is early and experimental.
