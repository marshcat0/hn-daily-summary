What is even worse, because I ship software from my computer, a single compromised dependency can lead to compromise of all the users of my software.<p>Containers and VMs are one solution, but for local work, they are often detrimental to productivity. It takes effort to configure a machine with all the tools and configs needed for productive work, but a container or a VM will be stripped of all these tools.
