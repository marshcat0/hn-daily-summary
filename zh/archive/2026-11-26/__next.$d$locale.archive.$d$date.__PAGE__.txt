For a one-time payment, your agent makes an MCP call, and you receive a notification in our mobile app. Once you swipe up in the app and approve with a passkey (or your device’s biometric method), we create a one-time permission that instructs your wallet to send $X to your recipient’s address.<p>Trusted contacts let your agent send money to people that you’ve previously authorized without explicit passkey approval. Generally, these are people that you’d trust to send your money back even if your agent made a mistake.<p>We’re also able to support sending money to someone off Versine using claim forwarding, where the user authorizes us to forward a specific amount to a future address (which we create once we verify a recipient&#x27;s OTP).<p>Param worked on scaling vector search at Databricks and harness evals at Glean.
